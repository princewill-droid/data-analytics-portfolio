Pharmacy Sales Analysis

📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes pharmacy sales data to understand product performance, revenue, profit, sales volume, and category performance.

The analysis was completed using Microsoft Excel, including formulas, PivotTables, charts, and an interactive-style dashboard.

The project demonstrates how data can be transformed into useful business insights that can support inventory and sales decisions.

---

🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project were to:

- Analyze pharmacy sales transactions.
- Identify products generating the highest revenue.
- Identify products generating the highest profit.
- Determine which products had the highest sales volume.
- Compare revenue and profit across product categories.
- Calculate total revenue and total profit.
- Calculate the overall profit margin.
- Present the findings using charts and a dashboard.

📋 Dataset

The dataset contains pharmacy sales transactions with the following fields:

- Date
- Product
- Category
- Quantity Sold
- Cost Price
- Selling Price
- Revenue
- Profit

The dataset contains sales records for six pharmacy products across different product categories.

---

📊 Analysis Performed

1. Product Performance

Product-level analysis was performed to compare:

- Revenue
- Profit
- Quantity sold

The analysis identified Amoxicillin as the product with the highest revenue and profit within this dataset.

2. Sales Volume Analysis

Quantity sold was analyzed to identify products with higher sales volumes.

Vitamin C recorded the highest quantity sold in the dataset.

3. Category Analysis

Revenue and profit were compared across pharmacy product categories to understand how different categories contributed to overall performance.

4. Profitability Analysis

Total revenue and total profit were calculated, followed by the overall profit margin:

Profit Margin = Total Profit ÷ Total Revenue × 100

37.89%

💡 Key Insights

Based on the analyzed dataset:

- The pharmacy generated ₦154,800 in total revenue.
- Total profit was ₦58,650.
- The overall profit margin was approximately 37.89%.
- Amoxicillin generated the highest revenue and profit among the products analyzed.
- Vitamin C recorded the highest quantity sold.
- Product categories contributed differently to overall revenue and profitability.
